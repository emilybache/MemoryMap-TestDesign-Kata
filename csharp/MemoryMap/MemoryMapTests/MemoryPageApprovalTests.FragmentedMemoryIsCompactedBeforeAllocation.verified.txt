When memory is fragmented into gaps that are individually too small for a new allocation, existing allocations should be moved (compacted) to open up a single contiguous, aligned block. Here, freeing the middle Byte leaves two small gaps too small to fit a Word on their own, so allocating a Word should shift the last Byte down to free up enough contiguous space.
